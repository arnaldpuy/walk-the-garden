# Walk the Garden

Interactive companions to:

Puy, A., Aguiló-Rivera, C., Linga, S. N., Clarke, N., Richards, O., Flinders, S. & Melsen, L. A. (2026).
*Fifty years of research have deepened uncertainties in global irrigation water withdrawals.* **Nature Water**.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s44221-026-00710-0

- `index.html` — **Walk the Garden**: design your own analysis fork by fork through the paper's 360 analytical pathways, get your verdict, then watch the other 359 bloom and sort into the reported outcome shares.
- `canopy.html` — **The Garden Canopy**: the same multiverse as a 3D canopy (three.js).
- `forking_paths.html` — the garden of forking paths vs a single analytical pipeline, explained.
- `iww_forking_paths.html` — the paper's 360-pathway design, animated.

Outcome shares (17% decrease · 40% increase · 34% unstable) are as reported in the paper; the assignment of outcomes to individual pathways in the interactive pages is an illustrative allocation consistent with those shares. Data and code for the paper itself: https://github.com/arnaldpuy/uncertainy_irrigation_modelling

Funded by UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) under the UK government's Horizon Europe funding guarantee — project DAWN (EP/Y02463X/1). Visual style follows https://www.dawnirrigation.com.
