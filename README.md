# Walk the Garden

Interactive companion to:

Puy, A., Aguiló-Rivera, C., Linga, S. N., Clarke, N., Richards, O., Flinders, S. & Melsen, L. A. (2026).
*Fifty years of research have deepened uncertainties in global irrigation water withdrawals.* **Nature Water**.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s44221-026-00710-0

Design your own analysis fork by fork through the paper's 360 analytical pathways, get your verdict, then watch the other 359 bloom and sort into the reported outcome shares.

Outcome shares (17% decrease · 40% increase · 34% unstable) are as reported in the paper; the assignment of outcomes to individual pathways is an illustrative allocation consistent with those shares. Data and code for the paper itself: https://github.com/arnaldpuy/uncertainy_irrigation_modelling

Funded by UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) under the UK government's Horizon Europe funding guarantee — project DAWN (EP/Y02463X/1). Visual style follows https://www.dawnirrigation.com.
